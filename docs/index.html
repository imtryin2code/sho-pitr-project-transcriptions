Joe Peter Project

Preserving the 1941 Chinook Jargon Transcriptions of Joe Peter and Jack Marr

Welcome to the Archive

In 1941, historical recordings were made on metal discs capturing a unique linguistic exchange. Jack Marr provided English prompts from Franz Boas's texts, and Joe Peter provided the Chinook Jargon translations. This site makes those transcriptions accessible to the community for the first time.
